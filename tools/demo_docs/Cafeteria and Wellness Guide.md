## Cafeteria menu and timings

The ground-floor cafeteria serves South Indian breakfast from 8:00 to 10:00 AM, a thali lunch from 12:30 to 2:30 PM and evening snacks from 4:00 to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Every Friday is **millet day**: the lunch thali is made with ragi and jowar.

Lunch costs INR 60 on the meal card. Tea, coffee, buttermilk and fruit are free all day.

## Special diets

Jain, vegan and gluten-free meals are available on request. Order by 11:00 AM on the same day using the Cafeteria app or by messaging the cafeteria manager, Suresh Babu, on extension 2290.

## Wellness room

A quiet wellness room on the 4th floor can be booked for 30 minutes at a time for rest, prayer or nursing mothers. Book it through the Outlook calendar as "Wellness Room 4F".

## Yoga and fitness

Free yoga classes run every Tuesday and Thursday from 7:30 to 8:15 AM on the terrace, led by a certified instructor. Bring your own mat. The annual Nimbus step challenge runs in January; the top three walkers win an extra day of earned leave.
