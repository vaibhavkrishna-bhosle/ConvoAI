# Travel and Expense Policy

Nimbus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. Version 3.0, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Finance. This policy explains what business expenses Nimbus pays for, the limits that apply, and how to claim them. It applies to all employees travelling or spending money on behalf of the company.

## 1. General principles

Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own. Only expenses that are necessary for business, supported by an original bill or e-invoice, and within the limits below are reimbursed. Expenses above the limits need written pre-approval from your department head; otherwise the excess is not reimbursed.

## 2. Booking travel

All flights, trains and hotels are booked through the Nimbus travel desk on PeopleHub under "Travel Request", at least 7 days before domestic travel and 21 days before international travel. Your manager approves the travel request. Bookings made directly by employees are reimbursed only if the travel desk could not book in time.

## 3. Travel class

| Grade | Flights (domestic) | Flights (international) | Train |
| L1 to L4 | Economy | Economy | AC 2-tier |
| L5 and L6 | Economy (premium economy for flights over 4 hours) | Premium economy | AC 1st class |
| L7 and above | Business | Business | AC 1st class |

## 4. Hotels

| City category | Hotel limit per night |
| Metro (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) | INR 7,500 |
| Other cities in India | INR 5,000 |
| International | USD 200 (or local equivalent) |

Use the company's partner hotels where available; the travel desk has negotiated rates.

## 5. Per diem (daily allowance)

When travelling outside your base city for business, you receive a per diem to cover meals, local snacks and tips, without needing bills:

| Location | Per diem |
| Metro cities in India | INR 2,500 per day |
| Other cities in India | INR 1,800 per day |
| International | USD 60 per day |

Per diem is not paid for days when meals are provided by the client or at a conference.

## 6. Local transport

Taxis and app cabs used for business are reimbursed at actuals with the ride receipt. Use of your own car or two-wheeler for business is reimbursed at INR 12 per km for cars and INR 5 per km for two-wheelers, based on the distance recorded in the claim. Daily commute between home and office is not reimbursable. Late-night cabs after 9:00 PM for employees working late are reimbursed at actuals.

## 7. Client entertainment

Client meals and entertainment must have a clear business purpose. The limit for client dinners and lunches is INR 2,000 per person (including Nimbus employees present), up to a maximum of INR 20,000 per event. Alcohol is reimbursed only for client entertainment and must not exceed 30 percent of the bill. Record the names and companies of all attendees in the claim. Events above INR 20,000 need prior approval from the VP of the department.

## 8. Non-reimbursable expenses

The following are never reimbursed: personal entertainment, minibar, laundry for trips shorter than 4 days, traffic fines, airline upgrades, travel insurance bought separately (Nimbus already covers business travel), and expenses for family members.

## 9. Submitting claims

Submit expense claims on PeopleHub under "Expenses" within 30 days of the expense, with photos or PDFs of all bills. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed. Your manager approves claims up to INR 50,000; claims above INR 50,000 also need the department head's approval. Approved claims are paid with the next salary run if approved by the 20th of the month.

## 10. Travel advances

For international travel or trips longer than 5 days, you may request a travel advance of up to 75 percent of the estimated expenses on PeopleHub. The advance must be settled with a claim within 15 days of returning.

## 11. Corporate cards

Employees in Sales, and employees in grade L5 and above who travel frequently, may be issued a corporate credit card. Card statements must be reconciled on PeopleHub every month; personal use is not permitted.
