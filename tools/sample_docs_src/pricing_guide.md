# NimbusDesk Product and Pricing Guide

For the Nimbus Sales team. Version 2026.2, effective 1 July 2026. Confidential: do not share this guide with customers; share the public pricing page instead.

## 1. Product overview

NimbusDesk is a cloud helpdesk that lets support teams manage customer conversations from email, chat, WhatsApp and phone in one shared inbox. Key capabilities are ticketing, automations, a self-service knowledge base, SLA tracking, reporting and integrations with popular CRMs. NimbusDesk is billed per agent (a user who handles tickets); light users who only view or comment are free.

## 2. Plans and list prices

All prices are per agent per month, in Indian rupees, excluding GST. Annual billing is paid upfront for 12 months.

| Plan | Billed annually | Billed monthly | Best for |
| Basic | INR 499 | INR 599 | Small teams starting with email ticketing |
| Professional | INR 999 | INR 1,199 | Growing teams that need automation and chat |
| Enterprise | INR 1,799 | INR 2,099 | Large or regulated teams with advanced security needs |

International customers are billed in US dollars: Basic USD 9, Professional USD 18 and Enterprise USD 32 per agent per month, billed annually.

## 5. Free trial

Every new customer can try the Professional plan free for 14 days with up to 10 agents, without a credit card. Trials can be extended once by 7 days by the account executive. At the end of the trial the account becomes read-only until a plan is purchased.

## 6. Discounts and approvals

Annual prices already include the annual-billing saving of about 17 percent compared with monthly prices. Additional discounts follow this approval matrix:

| Discount on annual list price | Who can approve |
| Up to 10 percent | Account Executive (no approval needed) |
| More than 10 and up to 20 percent | Sales Manager |
| More than 20 and up to 30 percent | VP Sales (Arjun Mehta) |
| More than 30 percent | CEO, with a written business case |

Special rules:

- Small businesses with fewer than 25 agents: maximum discount is 12 percent.
- Multi-year deals: an extra 5 percent for a 2-year prepaid contract and 10 percent for a 3-year prepaid contract, on top of the approved discount.
- Registered non-profits and educational institutions: a flat 25 percent discount, approved by the Sales Manager with proof of registration.
- Monthly plans are never discounted.

Record every discount and its approval in the CRM before sending the quotation.

## 7. Creating a quotation

1. Open the opportunity in Nimbus CRM and make sure the company, contact and number of agents are correct.
2. Click "Create Quote", choose the plan, billing term and add-ons.
3. Enter the discount. If it needs approval, the CRM routes it to the right approver automatically.
4. Once approved, generate the PDF quote and send it from the CRM so it is tracked.
5. Quotes are valid for 30 days.

## 8. Contract terms

Monthly plans have no minimum term and can be cancelled at any time, effective at the end of the current month. Annual plans have a minimum term of 12 months. Agents can be added at any time (prorated); agents can be removed only at renewal.

## 9. Refund and cancellation policy

New annual customers can cancel within 30 days of their first payment for a full refund (the 30-day money-back guarantee). After 30 days, annual plans are non-refundable, but the customer keeps access until the end of the paid term. Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. Refunds are processed by Finance within 10 business days to the original payment method.

How to handle a refund request: confirm the customer is within the 30-day window, understand the reason and try to resolve the issue (offer a call with Customer Success), then raise a "Refund Request" in the CRM with the reason. Refunds above INR 1,00,000 need the Sales Manager's approval.

## 10. Payment methods

Customers can pay by credit or debit card, UPI, net banking, or bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS) against an invoice. Enterprise customers may pay by invoice with 30-day payment terms. International customers pay by card or wire transfer in USD.

## 11. Escalating a customer complaint

1. Acknowledge the customer within 4 business hours and log the complaint in the CRM.
2. If it is a product or support issue, involve the Customer Success Manager (or support lead for non-Enterprise accounts).
3. If unresolved after 2 business days, escalate to the Sales Manager.
4. Complaints involving data security, legal threats or contracts above INR 25 lakh go directly to the VP Sales and the Legal team.

## 12. Sales commission

Account executives earn commission of 8 percent of first-year annual contract value (ACV) for new business, and 3 percent of the value of renewals and upsells. Above 100 percent of quarterly quota, the new-business rate rises to 12 percent (accelerator). Commissions are paid monthly, one month after the customer pays. If a customer cancels within 90 days, the commission is clawed back.
