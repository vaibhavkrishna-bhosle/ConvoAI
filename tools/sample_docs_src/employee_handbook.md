# Nimbus Technologies Employee Handbook

Version 4.2 - Effective 1 April 2026. Owner: People Operations (HR). This handbook applies to all full-time employees of Nimbus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. in India. Interns and contractors should read the section "Interns and Contract Staff" for the rules that apply to them. If anything in this handbook conflicts with your appointment letter, the appointment letter prevails.

## 1. Welcome to Nimbus

Nimbus Technologies builds NimbusDesk, a customer-support helpdesk used by more than 1,200 companies across India, South-East Asia and the Middle East. We are headquartered in Bengaluru and have offices in Pune and Hyderabad. This handbook explains how we work together: your working hours, leave, pay, benefits, how we review performance, and what we expect from each other.

Questions about anything in this handbook can be sent to your HR Business Partner (see section 13) or to hr@nimbus.example. All HR requests - leave, reimbursements, letters and personal details - are raised on PeopleHub, our HR portal at peoplehub.nimbus.example.

## 2. Working Hours and Attendance

Our standard working week is Monday to Friday, five working days, 40 hours a week. Core hours are 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM, when everyone is expected to be available for meetings. Outside core hours you may start between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM and finish accordingly, as long as you complete 8 hours a day.

Attendance is recorded automatically when you log in to PeopleHub or badge into the office. If you forget, you can regularise attendance on PeopleHub within 7 days; your manager approves it.

### Late coming policy

Joining after 10:30 AM without informing your manager counts as a late mark. Three late marks in a calendar month are converted into half a day of casual leave. Persistent late coming is discussed in your monthly one-on-one before any further action is taken. Late marks caused by documented transport disruptions (heavy rain alerts, strikes) are waived by HR.

### Overtime

Nimbus does not expect regular overtime. When work outside normal hours is required (for example a production incident or a release weekend), employees in grades L1 to L3 are paid overtime at 1.5 times their hourly basic rate for weekday hours beyond 9 hours and 2 times the hourly basic rate on weekends and public holidays. Overtime must be pre-approved by your manager on PeopleHub. Employees in grade L4 and above receive compensatory off (comp-off) instead of overtime pay.

### Shifts

Customer Support and Site Reliability teams work in three rotating shifts: Morning (6:00 AM - 3:00 PM), General (10:00 AM - 7:00 PM) and Night (9:00 PM - 6:00 AM). Night shift attracts a shift allowance of INR 400 per night. To change your shift, agree a swap with a colleague of the same role and submit a shift change request on PeopleHub at least 3 working days in advance; your team lead approves it.

## 3. Leave Policy

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Leave is credited monthly on PeopleHub and is pro-rated for employees who join during the year. All leave must be applied for on PeopleHub and approved by your manager before it is taken, except sick leave and emergencies, which can be applied for on the day you return.

| Leave type | Days per year | Carry forward | Notes |
| Casual leave | 12 | No | Max 3 days at a time; lapses on 31 December |
| Sick leave | 10 | Up to 20 days in total | Medical certificate needed for more than 2 consecutive days |
| Earned (privilege) leave | 18 | Up to 30 days in total | Apply 2 weeks in advance for 3 or more days |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks | - | For the first two children; 12 weeks thereafter |
| Paternity leave | 10 working days | - | Within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption |
| Adoption leave | 12 weeks | - | For a child below 3 months of age |
| Bereavement leave | 3 working days | - | Immediate family |
| Marriage leave | 5 working days | - | Once during employment |

### Casual leave

You get 12 days of casual leave per year (1 day credited each month). Casual leave is meant for short personal needs and can be taken for a maximum of 3 consecutive days. It cannot be combined with earned leave. Unused casual leave lapses at the end of the year and cannot be carried forward or encashed.

### Sick leave

You get 10 days of sick leave per year. Unused sick leave carries forward, up to a maximum balance of 20 days. For sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days, upload a medical certificate on PeopleHub within 3 days of returning to work.

### Earned leave, carry forward and encashment

You earn 18 days of earned leave per year (1.5 days per month). Up to 30 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year; anything above 30 days lapses on 31 December. Earned leave can be encashed only at the time of exit (see section 11), at your basic salary rate, up to a maximum of 30 days. Annual encashment while in service is not offered.

### Maternity, paternity and adoption leave

Women employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children and 12 weeks for each subsequent child, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date. After maternity leave, employees may work from home for up to 3 additional months with their manager's agreement.

Fathers receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption, in one stretch or in two blocks.

Employees adopting a child below 3 months of age receive 12 weeks of adoption leave.

### Public holidays

Nimbus observes 12 public holidays a year: 10 fixed holidays published by HR in December and 2 optional (floating) holidays you choose from the optional list. The holiday list for each office is available on PeopleHub under "Holiday Calendar". If you work on a public holiday at your manager's request, you receive a comp-off to be used within 60 days.

### Loss of pay

Leave taken without sufficient balance is treated as loss of pay (LOP) and deducted from that month's salary at the rate of one day's gross salary per day.

## 4. Hybrid Work and Work From Home

Nimbus follows a hybrid model. Employees attend the office three days a week - Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are our "together days". Monday and Friday are flexible: you may work from home or from the office. Teams may agree a different pattern with their department head, for example during a product launch.

### Working from home

When working from home you must be reachable during core hours, use the company VPN for internal systems, and work from a quiet, private space. Customer-facing calls must use a headset and a neutral background.

### Working from another city or country

You may work from another city in India for up to 30 days in a calendar year, with your manager's approval on PeopleHub at least one week in advance. Working remotely from outside India is not permitted, except for up to 10 working days a year with approval from your department head and HR, because of tax and data-protection rules. Longer remote arrangements need a formal Remote Work Agreement approved by the CHRO.

### Home office support

Every employee receives a one-time home office allowance of INR 10,000 for a chair, desk or monitor, claimed on PeopleHub with a bill within 6 months of joining.

## 5. Payroll and Salary

### Salary date

Salaries are credited to your bank account on the last working day of every month. If the last day of the month falls on a weekend or holiday, salary is credited on the previous working day. Payslips are published on PeopleHub under "My Pay" by the 2nd of the following month.

### Payslips, tax and certificates

Download payslips, your annual Form 16 and tax declarations from PeopleHub. Investment declarations for income tax must be submitted by 15 May and proofs by 31 January. You can request a salary certificate or an employment verification letter on PeopleHub under "Letters"; letters are issued within 3 working days.

### Updating bank details

To change the bank account your salary is paid into, update it on PeopleHub under "My Profile > Bank Details" and upload a cancelled cheque. Changes made after the 20th of a month apply from the following month's salary.

### Salary advance

In a genuine emergency you may request a salary advance of up to one month's net salary, once in a 12-month period. It is recovered in up to 6 equal monthly instalments without interest. Apply on PeopleHub; it needs approval from your manager and the Finance Controller.

### Bonus

Nimbus pays an annual performance bonus in June. Your target bonus is stated in your appointment letter as a percentage of annual fixed pay (10% for L1-L3, 15% for L4-L5, 20% for L6 and above). The actual payout is the target bonus multiplied by a company factor (0 to 1.2, based on company revenue achievement) and an individual factor (0 to 1.5, based on your performance rating). Employees who join after 1 January are paid a pro-rated bonus; employees serving notice on the payout date are not eligible.

### Payroll queries

For any salary, tax or payslip problem, raise a ticket on PeopleHub under "Payroll" or email payroll@nimbus.example. The payroll team responds within 2 working days. Urgent issues around salary credit can be escalated to the Payroll Lead, Kavya Iyer, at extension 2210.

## 6. Benefits

### Health insurance

All employees are covered by a group medical insurance policy with a family floater sum insured of INR 5,00,000 per year, covering you, your spouse and up to two children. You may add your parents or parents-in-law with a separate cover of INR 3,00,000, with the premium deducted from your salary. Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals; the insurer's helpline and e-card are available on PeopleHub under "Insurance".

To add your spouse after marriage, or a newborn child, update your dependants on PeopleHub within 30 days of the event and upload the marriage or birth certificate. After 30 days, dependants can be added only at the annual renewal in April.

We also provide term life insurance of 3 times annual fixed pay and personal accident insurance of INR 25,00,000 for every employee at no cost.

### Allowances

| Allowance | Amount | How to claim |
| Wellness and gym allowance | INR 1,500 per month | Claim on PeopleHub with gym, yoga or sports bills |
| Internet allowance | INR 1,000 per month | Paid automatically with salary |
| Meal card | INR 2,200 per month | Loaded on the meal card on the 1st |
| Phone allowance | INR 500 per month | Paid automatically with salary (L3 and above, and Sales) |
| Night shift allowance | INR 400 per night | Paid with salary based on roster |

Unused wellness allowance carries forward within the financial year but lapses on 31 March.

### Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of INR 25,000 per financial year for courses, certifications, books and conferences related to their role. Get your manager's approval on PeopleHub before you enrol, then claim with the invoice and completion certificate. If you leave within 12 months of a reimbursed certification costing more than INR 15,000, the amount is recovered from your final settlement.

### Employee referral bonus

When someone you refer joins Nimbus and completes 90 days, you receive a referral bonus: INR 25,000 for roles up to L3, INR 50,000 for L4 and L5, and INR 75,000 for L6 and above. Refer candidates through the "Refer a Friend" page on PeopleHub. Referrals for your own team's open role made by the hiring manager are not eligible.

### Employee wellbeing

Our Employee Assistance Programme offers free, confidential counselling for you and your family, 24x7, at 1800-200-4567. The office has a gym on the 3rd floor, open 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM on working days.

## 7. Onboarding

### Documents needed before joining

Please upload the following documents on the PeopleHub onboarding portal at least 3 days before your joining date:

- Government photo ID (passport, Aadhaar card or driving licence)
- PAN card
- Address proof (if different from your photo ID)
- Educational certificates and mark sheets from Class 10 onwards
- Relieving letter and experience letters from previous employers
- Last three months' payslips from your previous employer
- Bank account details with a cancelled cheque
- Two recent passport-size photographs
- Form 12B with income details from your previous employer for the current financial year

### Your first day

Your first day starts at 9:30 AM at the reception of your office. You will collect your ID card and laptop, attend a welcome session with HR, complete IT setup with the IT team, and have lunch with your team. Your manager will share a 30-60-90 day plan in your first week.

### Buddy programme

Every new joiner is paired with a buddy from their team for the first 60 days. Your buddy helps you find your way around tools, people and processes.

### Probation

New employees are on probation for 6 months. Your manager reviews your progress at 3 months and confirms your employment at the end of probation. Probation may be extended once by up to 3 months. During probation the notice period is 30 days.

## 8. Performance and Appraisal

### Appraisal cycle

Nimbus runs one annual appraisal cycle, from 1 April to 31 March. Goals are set in April, a mid-year check-in happens in October, and the annual review takes place in April. Salary revisions from the annual review take effect from 1 July. Employees who join after 1 January are reviewed in the following cycle.

### Ratings

Performance is rated on a five-point scale: 5 - Exceptional, 4 - Exceeds Expectations, 3 - Meets Expectations, 2 - Partially Meets Expectations, 1 - Does Not Meet Expectations. Ratings are calibrated across each department to keep them fair.

### Promotions

Promotions are decided once a year as part of the annual review. To be considered, you need at least 18 months in your current grade, a rating of 4 or 5 in the latest review, and a nomination from your manager supported by examples of work at the next level. Promotion nominations are reviewed by a panel of department heads and HR, and results are announced in June.

### Performance improvement

Employees rated 1, or rated 2 twice in a row, are placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan with clear goals and weekly support from their manager.

## 9. Code of Conduct

We expect everyone at Nimbus to act with integrity, treat colleagues and customers with respect, and protect company and customer information.

### Gifts from vendors and customers

You may accept gifts of nominal value up to INR 2,000 in total per year from any one vendor or customer, such as festival sweets or branded merchandise. Gifts above this value, cash, gift cards or travel must be politely declined or handed over to HR. Report any gift above INR 2,000 to compliance@nimbus.example.

### Conflict of interest and moonlighting

You must declare any personal or financial interest that could conflict with your work at Nimbus. Working for another employer, or for a business that competes with or sells to Nimbus, is not permitted. Other paid work outside office hours - such as teaching, writing or freelance projects unrelated to our business - needs written approval from HR and your department head before you start.

### Prevention of sexual harassment (POSH)

Nimbus has zero tolerance for sexual harassment. Complaints can be raised with the Internal Committee at ic@nimbus.example or with any committee member listed on PeopleHub. All complaints are handled confidentially and resolved within 90 days, as required by the POSH Act, 2013. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern is itself a violation of this code.

### Dress code

Our dress code is smart casual. On customer visits and at events, business casual is expected. Clothes with offensive slogans are not appropriate at work.

## 10. Travel and Expenses

Business travel, client entertainment and other expenses are governed by the separate Travel and Expense Policy. In summary: book travel through the travel desk, keep all bills, and submit claims on PeopleHub within 30 days. Please read the full Travel and Expense Policy for limits and approvals.

## 11. Leaving Nimbus

### Notice period

The notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees and 30 days during probation. You resign by submitting your resignation on PeopleHub; your manager and HR will discuss your last working day. Notice may be shortened at the company's discretion, or bought out by paying basic salary for the unserved days.

### Exit formalities

Before your last working day:

- Hand over your work and documentation to the person named by your manager
- Return your laptop, ID card, access card and any other company property to IT and Facilities
- Clear any outstanding advances or expense claims
- Complete the exit interview with HR
- Get clearance from your manager, IT, Finance and Admin on PeopleHub

### Full and final settlement and letters

Your full and final settlement - including salary for days worked, earned leave encashment and any deductions - is paid within 45 days of your last working day. Your relieving letter and experience letter are emailed within 7 working days of your last working day, once all clearances are complete.

## 12. Interns and Contract Staff

Interns receive a monthly stipend, 1 day of leave per month of internship, access to the cafeteria and gym, and a certificate on completion. Interns are not covered by group health insurance but are covered by personal accident insurance. Contract staff follow the leave and benefit terms of their agency contract.
