# About Nimbus Technologies

## Who we are

Nimbus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a software company that builds **NimbusDesk**, a customer-support helpdesk platform. Founded in 2016 in Bengaluru by Priya Raman and Vikram Shetty, Nimbus today has about 620 employees and serves more than 1,200 customers in India, South-East Asia and the Middle East.

## Mission and values

**Our mission:** help every business give its customers fast, human and helpful support.

Our core values are:

1. **Customer first** - we start with the customer's problem, not our product.
2. **Own it** - we take responsibility for outcomes, not just tasks.
3. **Be candid and kind** - we give honest feedback, respectfully.
4. **Keep learning** - we experiment, measure and improve.
5. **Win together** - we share credit and help each other succeed.

## Facilities

### Meeting rooms

Book meeting rooms through the Outlook calendar: add the room as a "location" when you create a meeting. Rooms are named after Indian rivers (Kaveri, Ganga, Narmada and so on). Rooms booked but not checked in within 10 minutes are released automatically. The large Godavari training room (40 seats) must be booked through admin@nimbus.example.

### Cafeteria

The cafeteria on each office's ground floor serves breakfast (8:00 to 10:00 AM), lunch (12:30 to 2:30 PM) and snacks (4:00 to 6:00 PM), Monday to Friday. It is closed on Saturdays and Sundays. Meals are paid with the meal card; tea, coffee and fruit are free all day.

### Parking

Car parking at the Bengaluru head office is allocated by the Admin team based on availability, with priority for employees with disabilities and expectant mothers. Two-wheeler parking is free and available to all. Register your vehicle number with the Admin desk to get a parking sticker. Visitor parking is available in basement B1 for up to 4 hours.

### Guests and visitors

Employees may bring guests to the office during office hours. Register your guest at least one day in advance on PeopleHub under "Visitor Registration". Guests must show photo ID at reception, wear a visitor badge, and be accompanied by you at all times. Guests are not allowed in server rooms or restricted areas.

## Our history

- **2016:** Nimbus founded in Bengaluru; first version of NimbusDesk launched.
- **2018:** Series A funding; Pune office opens.
- **2021:** WhatsApp channel launched; 500 customers reached.
- **2023:** Hyderabad office opens; Enterprise plan launched.
- **2025:** AI reply suggestions launched; 1,000 customers reached.
- **2026:** Expansion into the Middle East.
